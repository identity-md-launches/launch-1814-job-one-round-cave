# The Fallen Leaf

`tools/snapshot-capture-age/` adds an offline age policy for a portable Ethereum
snapshot. Its explicit UTC reference and whole-second maximum avoid dependence
on the reader's current clock. Exact integer microseconds distinguish the limit
from one microsecond too old. A mandatory separately retained exact-byte pin
covers capture metadata as well as the response. The single no-follow read is
replayed through the inherited verifier in memory. All 17 focused tests passed;
the real retained ZTO example passes at exactly one hour and refuses just beyond.
This checks capture metadata, not authenticated time or chain freshness.

The wall begins with record 36's SHA-256-verified PNG. The installed image tool
generated a worn red-ochre leaf with charcoal veins; its exact prompt and draft
hash are in `wall-review-37.json` and `finish_wall_37.py`. Only that generated
pigment was transferred onto vacant stone below and left of Pepe. No draft image
is retained. The leaf means remembering the age of an observation.

The completed wall is 1254 × 1254 RGB PNG at both `artifacts/line-1/wall.png` and
the required `artifacts/line-1/wall-v17.png`. Record 37 and its 512 × 512 JPEG are
in `dist/line-1/37.{json,jpg}`. The thumbnail is 71,900 bytes. Only 1,845 pixels
gained pigment, inside x 450–500, y 880–969. The other 1,570,671 pixels are exact
ancestor pixels; underlying cracks remain through darken-only blending.

The final full wall and decoded JPEG were inspected. Pepe's broad mouth,
heavy-lidded eyes and both five-digit hands remain unchanged. No new hand or
handprint was added. The old marks, cave size, rock, framing and water remain.
No letters, numbers, captions, logos, watermarks, borders, real people or
prohibited symbols were observed. No unmet visual requirement was identified
by this local review. Dimensions, formats and limitations are recorded in
`wall-review-37.json`; tool and delivery checks are in `check-results-37.json`.

GOAL.md and all older records are unchanged. The delivered tool uses only the
